SplitBill_EX

Tax, tip and card fees included — settled to the smallest unit.

Download on the App Store (free)

Splitting a bill abroad goes wrong for the same reasons every time. The currency is not yours, a card fee sits on top, tax and tip are added separately, and somebody paid for everyone. SplitBill_EX keeps all of that in the calculation and gives you the only answer that matters: who pays whom, and how much.

Screens

What it does

  • Fees in the right order — tax and tip are added first, then the card fee on top, the way the card company actually bills you. The order changes the total, so it matters
  • 30 currencies with live rates — pay in euros, settle in dollars
  • Nothing rounded on the way — amounts are held as decimals and rounded only per transfer and on output, so the parts still add up to the whole
  • Fewest transfers — debts are netted off before anyone pays
  • Leave out your own purchases — something you bought for yourself on the shared bill can be excluded, with the surcharge applied only to that part
  • Shared rooms — share a six-character room ID and everyone sees the same bills as they are entered

Privacy

Bills stay on your iPhone unless you use a shared room. In a shared room, participant names and bill contents are stored in a database run by the developer, and anyone who knows the room ID can read and write them. Please read the privacy policy before sharing a room.
