Counter1234

Counting, done properly — and nothing else.

Download on the App Store (free)

Counting stock, counting people through a door, counting laps. The hard part is usually that you have to look at the screen to press anything. Counter1234 counts with the volume buttons, so you can keep your eyes on what you are counting — or keep the phone in your pocket.

Screens

What it does

  • Count with the volume buttons — no need to look at the screen. Each press returns a haptic tap so you know it registered
  • Any step you like — the four buttons take any whole number, positive or negative. Count in 10s, in 500s, or downwards
  • 100 named memories — save a tally under a name and recall it later. Link one and it follows the count as you go
  • Undo — the last 50 changes can be taken back
  • Full screen — the number only, rotatable, large enough to read from across a room
  • CSV export — writes out the memory slots with their names and timestamps

Privacy

Everything stays on your iPhone. There is no account and no sign-in. See the privacy policy for details.
